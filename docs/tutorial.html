Before students arrive

Keep it easy to join

Allow thinking time and let students rehearse with a partner. Offer the sentence starters on every page. Students can stay seated for movement tasks and pass on personal questions.

In large classes, have groups present to neighbouring groups. Everyone gets a turn within the time.

Using the screen

Classroom view hides the teacher guide and enlarges the shared activity. Press it again to return. Your browser’s full-screen option can give you more room.

Secret cards: only reveal when the guesser or drawer is facing away. Hide the card before they turn back. Classroom view alone does not hide a revealed card.

What the buttons do

Vote buttons record totals entered on this device; they are not a live student poll. Ranking arrows let the teacher arrange a group’s list. Prompt buttons cycle through ready-made rounds.

Changing prompts resets that prompt’s answers and counters. Refreshing or leaving a page clears the activity and timer. Nothing is sent to a server.
