Activity 05 / 20 · Creative

Mystery Object

Practise describing, speculating and explaining through imaginative guesses.

Groups of 3–420 minutesOne everyday object
Homeroom timer
20:00

Set up & model

Choose a length, then start.

Let’s give it a go.

Round 1

Words to get you started

“It might be ___.” · “It could help you ___.” · “We think it is used for ___.”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Hold up a binder clip, whisk or another small classroom object. If students know its real use, challenge them to invent a surprising new one.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Look & wonder

    Show the object. Collect words for its shape, size and material without confirming a use.

  2. 3–9 min
    Generate ideas

    Each student proposes one possible use. Groups ask how each idea would work, then choose their favourite.

  3. 9–16 min
    Build a mini pitch

    Groups give the invention a name and prepare a 30-second explanation. Each member contributes one line and rehearses it.

  4. 16–20 min
    Pitch & choose

    Groups pitch to a neighbouring group. Hear one or two examples, then ask students to name the most imaginative use they heard.

English skills

Description • Speculation with might/could • Explaining a function

Adjust the challenge

More support: Offer word choices: round, metal, small, hold, open, carry.

More challenge: Add a limitation and explain how the invention could be improved.
