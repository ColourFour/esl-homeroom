Two Truths and a Lie: Topic Edition

Make sentence building and follow-up questions social through a light guessing game.

Round 1

Words to get you started

“How often do you ___?” · “When did you ___?” · “I think ___ is the lie because ___.”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Choose a topic and use the example sentences as models. Students create two true statements and one invented statement of their own. Personal details are optional; an invented character is fine. The page has no preset correct lie.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model the game

    Give three model statements: two true and one invented. Let students ask a question, guess, and hear your reveal.

  2. 3–9 min
    Write & rehearse

    Students write their own three statements. Help with wording and let them quietly practise reading them.

  3. 9–16 min
    Question & guess

    Take turns in groups. Each person reads their statements; classmates ask a question before guessing. The speaker then reveals the lie.

  4. 16–20 min
    Share & reflect

    Each student tells a partner one interesting true statement they heard. Invite volunteers to share a useful question.

English skills

Sentence construction • Follow-up questions • Listening for clues

Adjust the challenge

More support: Use present-tense frames: “I like / I can / I have…”

More challenge: Use past experiences and ask two questions before each guess.
