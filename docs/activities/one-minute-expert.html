Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Open a topic card. Give students scrap paper for keywords. Fluency matters more than perfect grammar; pauses and a shorter first attempt are welcome.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model a mini talk

    Give a 20-second talk using an opinion, a reason and an example. Show the topic and sentence starters.

  2. 3–9 min
    Prepare & practise

    Students note three keywords, then give a 30-second talk to a partner. The listener asks one friendly question. Swap roles.

  3. 9–16 min
    Meet a new listener

    Choose another topic or improve the first talk. Change partners and aim for 60 seconds each. Use the page’s one-minute timer for individual turns.

  4. 16–20 min
    Celebrate progress

    Partners name one clear idea they heard. Invite a few volunteers to repeat a favourite line and identify what helped them keep talking.

English skills

Fluency • Organising ideas • Giving examples

Adjust the challenge

More support: Allow 20–30 seconds and display three useful words.

More challenge: Ask the listener to pose an unexpected follow-up question.
