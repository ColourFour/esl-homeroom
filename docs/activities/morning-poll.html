Words to get you started

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Read the choices and count hands, or let students move to labelled areas. Use the vote buttons to enter class totals; students may stay seated.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Vote once

    Read the question and explain each choice. Count one vote per student, entering the totals on the page.

  2. 3–9 min
    Explain your choice

    In pairs, students explain their vote and ask a follow-up question. Join another pair and compare reasons.

  3. 9–16 min
    Read the results

    Reveal the chart. Groups describe the most and least popular options, then suggest a reason for the pattern. Vote again only if you want a before/after comparison.

  4. 16–20 min
    Report a finding

    Each group prepares one sentence about the results. Hear a few examples; partners share one surprising opinion.

Adjust the challenge

More support: Use “More students chose ___ than ___.”

More challenge: Estimate proportions and ask what another class might choose.
