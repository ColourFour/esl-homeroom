Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Only the clue-giver may see the card: angle the device away from guessers or ask guessers to face away. The class-facing view keeps words hidden until you reveal them. Use paper slips for simultaneous group play.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model & prepare

    Demonstrate pizza: “It is usually round. You cut it into slices.” Explain: no target word, word parts, translations or gestures.

  2. 3–9 min
    Play the first round

    One student describes a word for up to 45 seconds while others guess. Rotate the clue-giver after each turn. Keep turns short so everyone speaks.

  3. 9–16 min
    Make & play new cards

    Each student writes a familiar target word with two banned words. Shuffle the new cards and play again, changing the clue-giver every turn.

  4. 16–20 min
    Collect good clues

    Each group shares one effective description. Everyone tells a partner another way to explain a difficult word.

English skills

Vocabulary recall • Paraphrasing • Listening for meaning

Adjust the challenge

More support: Ban only one word and allow a pass after a good attempt.

More challenge: Ban three words or require two different clues before a guess.
