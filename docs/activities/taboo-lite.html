Taboo Lite

Groups of 3–4

Timer ↓Prompt 1
  1. 1Guessers look away
  2. 2Give clues in English
  3. 3Guess before time runs out

A few useful phrases

“It is a kind of ___.” · “You use it to ___.” · “It is similar to ___.”

Teacher guide Setup · 20-minute plan · English support

Explain a word without saying the obvious clues. Useful practice for when students know what they mean but can’t find a word.

You need: Screen or word cards

Before you start

Only the clue-giver should see the card. Keep the guessers facing away from the screen. For several groups at once, copy a few cards onto paper.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Give a quick example

    For pizza, try “It is usually round. You cut it into slices.” Explain that they can’t say the target word, the banned words or a translation.

  2. 3–9 min
    Play a few turns

    Give each clue-giver up to a minute. The others guess. Change the clue-giver after every word so everyone has a turn.

  3. 9–16 min
    Make new cards

    Each student writes a familiar word and two words the clue-giver can’t use. Shuffle and play another round.

  4. 16–20 min
    Collect useful clues

    Ask which descriptions worked. Choose a difficult word and see whether the class can explain it another way.

Worth keeping in mind

If the word itself is unfamiliar, change it. The point is finding another way to say something.

The English they’re using

Explaining vocabulary · Describing a function · Listening for meaning

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Ban just one word and allow a pass after an attempt.

Take it further: Ban three words or ask for a second description of the same word.
