Round 1

Words to get you started

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Write the two word sets below. Explain that different answers can work when the reason fits the words.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Choose & model

    Show round 1. Model “Eagle, because it can fly.” Ask whether someone can defend a different answer.

  2. 3–9 min
    Discuss alternatives

    Students choose privately, then take turns giving reasons in groups. Challenge each group to find at least two defensible answers.

  3. 9–16 min
    Try & create

    Discuss round 2. Then each group makes a new set of four familiar words and swaps it with a neighbouring group to solve.

  4. 16–20 min
    Share the reasoning

    Hear a few different answers. Each student tells a partner which explanation was most convincing and why.

English skills

Reasoning • Explaining categories • Justifying opinions

Adjust the challenge

More support: Start with familiar pictures or words and the frame “It can / has / is…”

More challenge: Find a convincing reason for every item in one set.
