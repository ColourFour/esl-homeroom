Odd One Out

Choose which word doesn’t belong and explain your rule. A different answer can be just as good.

Groups of 3–4No materials
Time left
20:00

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.

On the board

Prompt 1

A few useful phrases

“___ is different because ___.” · “The others all ___.” · “Another answer could be ___.”

How to run it

Before you start

Show one set of four words. Tell students that they need a reason for their answer. Keep the suggested reasons hidden until they have discussed it.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Give one answer

    For dog, eagle, shark and tiger, try “Eagle, because it can fly.” Ask whether a different answer could work.

  2. 3–9 min
    Find another reason

    Students choose privately, then explain their answers in groups. Ask each group to find at least two answers it can defend.

  3. 9–16 min
    Make a set

    Try the next prompt. Then groups write four words of their own and swap with another group to discuss.

  4. 16–20 min
    Hear the explanations

    Take a few answers and ask which rule each group used. Partners choose the explanation they found clearest.

Worth keeping in mind

Wait before revealing the examples. Once there’s an answer on the board, they may stop looking for their own.

The English they’re using

Grouping vocabulary · Giving reasons · Explaining a different view

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Check the word meanings and offer “The others all…”

Take it further: Find a reason for every word to be the odd one out.
