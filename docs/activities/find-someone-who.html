Words to get you started

“Do you like ___?” · “Can you ___?” · “What kind of ___?”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Write the five prompts below. Students copy short labels into notebooks and leave space for names. If moving is difficult, rotate partners at desks.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model an exchange

    Turn “likes spicy food” into “Do you like spicy food?” Model “Can you swim?” and “Do you have a pet?” Explain: ask aloud before writing a name.

  2. 3–9 min
    Meet classmates

    Students ask different classmates questions and record a name for each “yes.” Encourage full answers; nobody has to complete every prompt.

  3. 9–16 min
    Ask a little more

    Continue with new partners. Add a follow-up such as “What do you draw?” or “What is your pet’s name?” Record one detail.

  4. 16–20 min
    Report back

    In pairs, each student shares two discoveries: “Mina likes drawing. She draws animals.” Invite a few volunteers to share.

English skills

Question forms • Listening for details • Reporting information

Adjust the challenge

More support: Write a complete question beside each prompt.

More challenge: Ask for a different name on every line and two follow-up details.
