Describe & Draw

Pairs

Timer ↓Prompt 1
  1. 1Partner B looks away
  2. 2A studies the picture
  3. 3Hide it, then describe

A few useful phrases

“Draw ___ next to ___.” · “On the left or the right?” · “Could you repeat that?”

Teacher guide Setup · 20-minute plan · English support

One student describes a hidden picture. Their partner draws what they hear. Compare the results, then swap.

You need: Paper + pencils

Before you start

Pairs need paper and pencils. Let the describers study the secret picture while the drawers face away. Hide it before they turn back. The drawing pad is useful for a class example.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Make the task clear

    Model “Draw a circle above a square.” Explain that the describer can talk, but can’t point at the partner’s paper.

  2. 3–9 min
    Describe and draw

    Show scene A to the describers, then hide it. They describe the scene while their partners draw and ask questions. Reveal it to compare.

  3. 9–16 min
    Swap roles

    Use scene B. The new describer gives the instructions. Ask pairs to notice which words made the second drawing easier to follow.

  4. 16–20 min
    Compare what happened

    Each pair shares one helpful instruction or one misunderstanding. Put a useful phrase on the board for next time.

Worth keeping in mind

The drawing doesn’t have to be good. If the cat ends up under the house, work out which instruction led to it.

The English they’re using

Position words · Describing size and number · Asking for clarification

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Start with three objects. Check above, below, left and right first.

Take it further: Ask the describer to add sizes, colours or extra details.
