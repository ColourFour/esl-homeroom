Describe & Draw

Round 1

Words to get you started

“Draw ___ next to ___.” · “On the left or the right?” · “Could you repeat that?”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Pairs need paper and pencils. Turn the screen away from drawers while describers read the secret scene, then hide it before describing. Use the on-screen drawing pad for a class demonstration; pairs draw on paper.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Prepare & model

    Assign A and B. Give students two minutes to sketch their scene privately. Model “Draw a circle above the square.” Explain: no peeking or pointing.

  2. 3–9 min
    Describe scene A

    A describes their sketch; B draws on fresh paper without looking at A’s original. B asks clarification questions. Compare pictures at the end.

  3. 9–16 min
    Swap roles

    B describes their sketch while A draws. Compare, then discuss which instructions were clear and which needed more detail.

  4. 16–20 min
    Notice useful language

    Each pair chooses one helpful instruction to share. Students say one sentence describing a difference between the drawings.

English skills

Prepositions • Precise descriptions • Clarification questions

Adjust the challenge

More support: Start with three objects and review above, below, left and right.

More challenge: Add sizes, numbers and overlapping objects to the private sketches.
