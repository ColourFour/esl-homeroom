Sell Me This

Groups of 3–4

Timer ↓Prompt 1
  1. 1Meet your customer
  2. 2Choose two selling points
  3. 3Make a 30-second advert

A few useful phrases

“You need this because ___.” · “It is the most ___.” · “With this, you can ___.”

Teacher guide Setup · 20-minute plan · English support

Make a 30-second advertisement for something ordinary. Give people a reason to want it.

You need: One object per group

Before you start

Give each group a pencil, spoon, sock or notebook, or use the object named on screen. Read the customer brief before students decide what to sell.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Show them the idea

    Pick up an object and give a quick sales pitch. Invented features are fine, as long as students explain what they do.

  2. 3–9 min
    Decide what to sell

    Groups choose a name, a customer and two reasons to buy the product. Everyone suggests a benefit.

  3. 9–16 min
    Rehearse the advert

    Put the ideas into a 30-second advert. Give each student a line and practise saying it clearly.

  4. 16–20 min
    Try it on an audience

    Perform to a neighbouring group. Listeners say which feature would persuade them to buy it.

Worth keeping in mind

A pencil is enough. The interest comes from what they claim it can do.

The English they’re using

Describing benefits · Using adjectives · Persuading a listener

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Use a name, two features and “Try it today.”

Take it further: Have the audience raise an objection for the sellers to answer.

Time left
20:00

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.
