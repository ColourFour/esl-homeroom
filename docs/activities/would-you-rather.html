Would You Rather?

Get everyone talking by choosing between two playful options and explaining why.

Pairs → groups of 420 minutesNo materials
Homeroom timer
20:00

Set up & model

Choose a length, then start.

Let’s give it a go.

Round 1

Words to get you started

“I’d rather ___ because ___.” · “Why would you choose ___?”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Read out or write the three choices below. Students can point left or right, or move to either side of the room.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Choose & model

    Read the first choice. Model a short answer with “because.” Give students thinking time, then let them choose a side.

  2. 3–9 min
    Talk in pairs

    Both partners explain their choices and ask “Why?” Repeat with the second choice; change partners if practical.

  3. 9–16 min
    Try to persuade

    Use the third choice in groups of four. Each student gives a reason, then responds to someone else. Invite students to change their minds.

  4. 16–20 min
    Share & close

    Hear two or three contrasting reasons. Everyone tells a partner which argument they liked best.

English skills

Speaking • Giving reasons • Asking follow-up questions

Adjust the challenge

More support: Offer “I choose ___ because it is fun / useful / exciting.”

More challenge: Ask students to explain one disadvantage of their own choice.
