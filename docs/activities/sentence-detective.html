Words to get you started

“We changed ___ to ___ because ___.” · “The clue is ___.”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Display one sentence puzzle at a time. Students need paper or notebooks. Reveal the suggested answer only after partners discuss; other clear rewrites may also work.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Notice the clue

    Read the first sentence. Model how to notice time words, agreement or a missing question word without revealing the answer.

  2. 3–9 min
    Investigate together

    Pairs write a corrected version and explain their change. Reveal the suggested answer and compare it with their ideas.

  3. 9–16 min
    Solve more cases

    Work through the next puzzles. Join another pair to compare answers before revealing. Ask students to create one simple puzzle for the other pair.

  4. 16–20 min
    Explain the fix

    Each student explains one change to a partner. Invite two examples, focusing on useful patterns rather than naming grammar rules.

English skills

Sentence accuracy • Explaining corrections • Reading for clues

Adjust the challenge

More support: Give two possible corrections and let students choose with a reason.

More challenge: Ask for two different correct rewrites with the same meaning.
