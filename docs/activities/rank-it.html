Rank It

Build comparison and negotiation skills while agreeing on one shared ranking.

Groups of 3–420 minutesBoard + paper
Homeroom timer
20:00

Set up & model

Choose a length, then start.

Let’s give it a go.

Round 1

Words to get you started

“___ is more useful than ___ because ___.” · “I disagree because ___.”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Write the island challenge and items below on the board. Each group needs one sheet of paper and a pen.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Set the challenge

    Read the scenario and check the item names. Students privately choose their most useful item.

  2. 3–9 min
    Compare choices

    Each student names their top item and gives a reason. Groups discuss which items matter most and why.

  3. 9–16 min
    Agree on a ranking

    Groups write one shared list. Everyone must suggest or challenge a position. Ask them to prepare reasons for their first and last choices.

  4. 16–20 min
    Compare & reflect

    Two or three groups share their top choice. Partners explain one difference between their rankings.

English skills

Comparatives • Negotiation • Listening and responding

Adjust the challenge

More support: Rank just four items and offer “We put ___ first because ___.”

More challenge: Change the setting to a snowy mountain and reconsider one item.
