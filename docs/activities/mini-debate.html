Mini Debate

Take a side on a school issue, prepare a couple of reasons and respond to someone who disagrees.

Pairs → groups of 4No materials
Time left
20:00

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.

On the board

Prompt 1

A few useful phrases

“I agree because ___.” · “I see your point, but ___.” · “For example, ___.”

How to run it

Before you start

Show one statement. Students choose a side. If almost everyone agrees, invite a few volunteers to argue the other case for practice.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Choose a side

    Read the statement and model a reason. Give them a polite way to disagree: “I see your point, but…”

  2. 3–9 min
    Prepare two reasons

    Students work with someone on the same side. Think of two reasons and a concrete example, then practise explaining them.

  3. 9–16 min
    Discuss both sides

    Join another pair, ideally with a different view. Take turns giving reasons and answering each other. Everyone asks or answers a question.

  4. 16–20 min
    Reconsider

    Ask which reason from the other side was strongest. Take another show of hands to see whether anyone changed their mind.

Worth keeping in mind

One clear reason is better than a long list they can’t explain. Ask for an example.

The English they’re using

Giving arguments · Disagreeing politely · Responding to a point

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Give a reason to finish: “A later start would help because…”

Take it further: Finish by agreeing on a compromise.
