Words to get you started

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Write the statement below. Students choose agree or disagree; if views are one-sided, invite volunteers to try the other side for practice.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Choose & model

    Read the statement and let students choose a side. Model a reason and the response “I see your point, but…”

  2. 3–9 min
    Prepare reasons

    In same-side pairs, students prepare two arguments and one example. Each partner practises explaining a reason.

  3. 9–16 min
    Exchange views

    Join with an opposite-side pair where possible. Everyone speaks for about 30 seconds. Take turns responding, then ask a question of the other side.

  4. 16–20 min
    Reflect & close

    Students say which opposing reason was strongest. Take a quick vote to see whether anyone changed their mind.

English skills

Argument • Respectful disagreement • Listening and responding

Adjust the challenge

More support: Offer reasons to complete: “Students will have more time to ___.”

More challenge: Finish by suggesting a compromise that addresses both sides.
