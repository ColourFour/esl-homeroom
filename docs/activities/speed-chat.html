Minimal-prep setup

Arrange facing pairs or ask students to change partners at their desks. Use the conversation cards and one-minute turn timer. Passing on a personal question is fine.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model an exchange

    Ask a question, answer it and add one follow-up. Explain that both partners need a turn.

  2. 3–9 min
    Two conversation rounds

    Show a question. Give each student a minute to answer and respond. Change partners and choose the next question; allow time to move.

  3. 9–16 min
    Keep the conversation going

    Run two more rounds with new questions. Challenge each partner to ask a follow-up that starts with why, how or what.

  4. 16–20 min
    Remember & share

    Each student shares an interesting idea they heard without sharing private information. Partners identify a useful follow-up question.

English skills

Turn-taking • Follow-up questions • Listening for detail

Adjust the challenge

More support: Let pairs see the next question early and plan a few words.

More challenge: Ask two follow-ups before giving your own answer.
