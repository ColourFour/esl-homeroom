Picture Story

Turn unrelated pictures into a short story with a clear sequence and a surprise.

Groups of 3–420 minutesUse the pictures below

Set up & model

Choose a length, then start.

Let’s give it a go.

Round 1

Words to get you started

“First, ___.” · “Then, ___.” · “Suddenly, ___!” · “Finally, ___.”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Show the three picture cues below, or quickly sketch them on the board. Each group needs only a pen and paper for notes.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Name & imagine

    Check the three picture words. Model a first sentence and review first, then and finally.

  2. 3–9 min
    Build the plot

    Each student suggests an event. Groups agree on a beginning, problem and ending that use all three pictures.

  3. 9–16 min
    Rehearse together

    Prepare a story lasting about 60 seconds. Give everyone at least one line, then rehearse with a neighbouring group if time allows.

  4. 16–20 min
    Tell & respond

    Pair groups to tell their stories to each other. Listeners name their favourite moment and ask one question.

English skills

Sequencing • Narrative language • Collaborative speaking

Adjust the challenge

More support: Use three simple sentences; present tense is fine.

More challenge: Include dialogue, a reason with because, and an unexpected ending.
