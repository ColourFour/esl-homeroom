Picture Story

Groups of 3–4

Timer ↓Prompt 1
  1. 1Link all three pictures
  2. 2Give the story a problem
  3. 3Tell it together

A few useful phrases

“First, ___.” · “Then, ___.” · “Suddenly, ___!” · “Finally, ___.”

Teacher guide Setup · 20-minute plan · English support

Three pictures, one short story. Groups find a way to connect them and tell it in about a minute.

You need: Pictures on screen

Before you start

Show the three pictures and check the names. Groups can use paper for a few notes, but don’t need to write out the whole story.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Start them off

    Name the pictures and give a possible opening sentence. Leave the rest of the story to them.

  2. 3–9 min
    Put a story together

    Each person suggests something that happens. Groups agree on a beginning, a problem and an ending that use all three pictures.

  3. 9–16 min
    Practise telling it

    Give everyone a line. Rehearse once and keep it to about a minute. If it’s too long, decide which parts they can leave out.

  4. 16–20 min
    Tell another group

    Neighbouring groups tell each other their stories. Listeners say which part they liked and ask a question.

Worth keeping in mind

Let the story be a bit ridiculous. They still need to make the connection clear to the listener.

The English they’re using

Putting events in order · Telling a story · Linking ideas

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Use three sentences starting with first, then and finally.

Take it further: Add a line of dialogue and an unexpected ending.

Time left
20:00

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.
