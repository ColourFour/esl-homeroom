Minimal-prep setup

Each team needs paper. Display a category and use the one-minute timer for the sprint. Speed is playful; clear explanations earn as much attention as long lists.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model the rules

    Name two examples for the category. Explain: write different words, avoid repeats, and be ready to explain them.

  2. 3–9 min
    Sprint & explain

    Start a one-minute round. Teams write words together, then take turns explaining one word each. Invite the class to check uncertain entries.

  3. 9–16 min
    Try a new category

    Choose another category and repeat. Each team picks three useful words and makes a sentence for each; everyone contributes.

  4. 16–20 min
    Share discoveries

    Hear a useful word from each team and ask for a meaning or example. Students tell a partner one new word they will remember.

English skills

Vocabulary recall • Categorising • Defining words

Adjust the challenge

More support: Give two starter words and allow drawings before naming them.

More challenge: Only accept words the team can define without translating.
