Words to get you started

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Display a starter word. Groups can speak around a circle or use the shared chain on the page as a class. No elimination: offer help when someone gets stuck.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model a connection

    Show a seed word and add a linked word: “Rain → umbrella, because it keeps you dry.” Explain that a reason makes the connection work.

  2. 3–9 min
    Build a chain

    Each student adds a new word and a reason. Go around at least twice. The next word must connect to the most recent word.

  3. 9–16 min
    Try a new route

    Start with a new seed and use the optional target word as a destination. Work together to reach it in five links, explaining every connection.

  4. 16–20 min
    Trace the thinking

    Groups share their most surprising link. Partners retell part of their chain using because.

English skills

Vocabulary associations • Giving reasons • Listening and responding

Adjust the challenge

More support: Offer a category or two choices when a student needs help.

More challenge: Reach the target word in exactly five links without repeating a word.
