Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Display a story opening. Students sit in a circle or group. The story must stay school-appropriate and include everybody’s ideas.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Start the story

    Read the opening and model one sentence that continues it. Explain that each turn should connect to the previous sentence.

  2. 3–9 min
    Go around twice

    Each student adds one sentence in turn. Complete two rounds. Students may ask for a word or use a sentence starter.

  3. 9–16 min
    Add a twist

    Reveal the surprise cue. Continue for two more rounds, then work together to give the story an ending. Rehearse a short retelling.

  4. 16–20 min
    Retell & reflect

    Groups tell a neighbouring group their story. Listeners identify the twist and a detail they remembered.

English skills

Listening • Linking ideas • Narrative language

Adjust the challenge

More support: Accept short sentences and keep three sequence words visible.

More challenge: Require the next speaker to reuse a detail from the previous turn.
