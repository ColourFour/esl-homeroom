Story Chain

Build a story round the group, one sentence at a time. Each person has to listen before adding their part.

Groups of 4No materials

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.

On the board

Prompt 1

A few useful phrases

“Then ___.” · “But suddenly ___.” · “Because of that, ___.” · “In the end, ___.”

How to run it

Before you start

Groups sit where they can hear each other. Show an opening line. They can tell the story aloud; the box on the page is there for a shared class example.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Start a story

    Read the opening and add one sentence. Explain that each new sentence needs to connect to what came before.

  2. 3–9 min
    Go round twice

    Everyone adds a sentence in turn. Keep going for two rounds. Help with a missing word if someone gets stuck.

  3. 9–16 min
    Add a problem

    Reveal the extra detail and continue the story. Go round again, then decide how it ends. Practise a short retelling.

  4. 16–20 min
    Tell it to someone else

    Groups retell the story to their neighbours. Listeners identify the problem and a detail they remember.

Worth keeping in mind

Watch for one confident student taking over the plot. Give everyone a turn, even if they only add a short sentence.

The English they’re using

Listening to other speakers · Linking sentences · Telling a story

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Keep then, because and finally on the board.

Take it further: Make each speaker refer to a detail from the previous sentence.
