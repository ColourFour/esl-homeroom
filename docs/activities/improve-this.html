Words to get you started

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

Display one bad idea. Groups need paper for three improvements. Treat the idea as a playful design problem.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Spot the problem

    Read the bad idea aloud. Model one helpful suggestion with “We should…”

  2. 3–9 min
    Suggest fixes

    Each student suggests an improvement. Groups discuss who each suggestion helps and why.

  3. 9–16 min
    Make a better version

    Groups agree on three changes and prepare a 45-second pitch. Each member contributes a line; use the reveal button for an optional extra constraint.

  4. 16–20 min
    Share & respond

    Pair groups to present their fixes. Listeners choose the most useful change and explain their choice.

Adjust the challenge

More support: Offer a choice between two possible fixes before asking for a new idea.

More challenge: Keep one awkward constraint and explain the trade-off.
