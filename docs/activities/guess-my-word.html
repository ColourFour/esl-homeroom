Guess My Word

Ask yes/no questions to work out a hidden word. Start broad, then narrow it down.

Groups of 3–4Screen or word cards
Time left
20:00

Explain & model

Start when the class is ready.

On the board

Prompt 1

A few useful phrases

“Is it alive?” · “Can you eat it?” · “Do people use it at school?”

How to run it

Before you start

Turn the guesser away while the rest of the group reads the word. Hide it before they turn back. Use paper cards if several groups are playing at once.

The 20 minutes

  1. 0–3 min
    Model a few questions

    Show how “Is it alive?” rules out several possibilities. Put “Is it…?”, “Can it…?” and “Do people…?” on the board.

  2. 3–9 min
    Play and swap

    Give each guesser up to ten questions or two minutes, then a final guess. Reveal the word and change the guesser.

  3. 9–16 min
    Keep everyone involved

    Continue until everyone has had a turn. Ask the group to notice which questions helped most. Add familiar words on paper if needed.

  4. 16–20 min
    Keep the useful questions

    Hear a good question from each group. Ask students to improve a question that didn’t give them much information.

Worth keeping in mind

If they keep guessing individual words, stop briefly and model a question that rules out a whole category.

The English they’re using

Yes/no questions · Describing categories · Using answers to narrow a guess

Make it easier or harder

Keep it simple: Tell the guesser the category and leave question starters visible.

Take it further: Try a six-question limit.
