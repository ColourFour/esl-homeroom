Words to get you started

“Is it alive?” · “Can you eat it?” · “Do people use it at school?”

Teacher’s pocket guide

Choose. Model. Go.

Minimal-prep setup

The guesser faces away while the group reads the secret word. Hide the word before the guesser turns back. Use the question counter for a class demonstration; use paper cards for simultaneous group play.

Your 20-minute plan

  1. 0–3 min
    Model questions

    Demonstrate with a hidden word. Model “Is it…?”, “Can it…?” and “Do people use it…?” Explain that answers are yes or no.

  2. 3–9 min
    Play & rotate

    A guesser asks up to ten yes/no questions, then makes a guess. After two minutes or a correct answer, reveal the word and rotate.

  3. 9–16 min
    Continue with new words

    Keep rotating until everyone has been the guesser. Students make extra familiar-word cards if needed. Encourage broad questions before specific guesses.

  4. 16–20 min
    Review strategies

    Groups share their most useful question. Everyone writes or says one improved question they could use next time.

English skills

Yes/no questions • Categorising vocabulary • Deductive listening

Adjust the challenge

More support: Tell the guesser the category and display three question frames.

More challenge: Use a less obvious word or reduce the limit to six questions.
