01Opinions

Would You Rather?

Choose between two options, then try to convince a partner. The reason matters more than the choice.

Pairs → groups of 4 · No materials→
02Discussion

Rank It

Give each group a list to put in order. They have to agree, which gives them something to discuss.

Groups of 3–4 · Paper + pens→
03Move & talk

Find Someone Who…

Students move around asking short questions and finding things out about their classmates.

Class mingle · Notebooks→
04Stories & ideas

Describe & Draw

One student describes a hidden picture. Their partner draws what they hear. Compare the results, then swap.

Pairs · Paper + pencils→
05Stories & ideas

Mystery Object

Give an ordinary object a new purpose. Groups decide what it does and explain how someone would use it.

Groups of 3–4 · One small object→
06Questions

Two Truths and a Lie

Students write three statements about themselves. Classmates ask questions before guessing which one is made up.

Groups of 3–4 · Paper + pens→
07Vocabulary

Taboo Lite

Explain a word without saying the obvious clues. Useful practice for when students know what they mean but can’t find a word.

Groups of 3–4 · Screen or word cards→
08Stories & ideas

Picture Story

Three pictures, one short story. Groups find a way to connect them and tell it in about a minute.

Groups of 3–4 · Pictures on screen→
09Short talks

Sell Me This

Make a 30-second advertisement for something ordinary. Give people a reason to want it.

Groups of 3–4 · One object per group→
10Opinions

Mini Debate

Take a side on a school issue, prepare a couple of reasons and respond to someone who disagrees.

Pairs → groups of 4 · No materials→
11Vocabulary

Guess My Word

Ask yes/no questions to work out a hidden word. Start broad, then narrow it down.

Groups of 3–4 · Screen or word cards→
12Discussion

Odd One Out

Choose which word doesn’t belong and explain your rule. A different answer can be just as good.

Groups of 3–4 · No materials→
13Short talks

One-Minute Expert

Give students a familiar topic and a little thinking time, then ask them to talk for up to a minute.

Pairs · Paper for keywords→
14Discussion

Improve This

Start with an obviously bad school idea. Groups make three changes and explain why their version is better.

Groups of 3–4 · Paper + pens→
15Move & talk

Morning Poll

Take a quick class vote, discuss the choices, then use the results to practise comparisons.

Class → small groups · No materials→
16Stories & ideas

Story Chain

Build a story round the group, one sentence at a time. Each person has to listen before adding their part.

Groups of 4 · No materials→
17Questions

Speed Chat

Short conversations with a few different classmates. The follow-up question is what keeps each one going.

Pairs, then rotate · No materials→
18Vocabulary

Category Sprint

Teams list words in a category for one minute, then explain a few of the words they chose.

Teams of 3–4 · Paper + pens→
19Discussion

Sentence Detective

Find what needs changing in a sentence, agree on a better version and explain the change.

Pairs → groups of 4 · Paper + pens→
20Vocabulary

Word Connections

Link one word to the next and explain each connection. See where the group ends up.

Groups of 3–4 · No materials→
A couple of things to keep in mind

Give them a moment to think before asking for an answer. Make sure everyone gets a turn. Help with a missing word when they need it, but keep the conversation moving.
